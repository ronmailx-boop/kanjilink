## Script
You look at your phone all day. What if every glance taught you a kanji? Meet Kanjilink. One Japanese kanji, right on
your lock screen, in a home-screen widget, and in a gentle notification every few hours. You see its meaning and both
readings, with romaji, so you can start from zero. Tap "I know it", and it comes back tomorrow. Tap "Again", and you'll
see it in ten minutes. Over two thousand kanji, from JLPT N5 to N1. No account. No ads. Just learning, every time you
look. Kanjilink. Free on Google Play.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the Kanjilink Android app (“the app”), published by Vplus Studio (“we”), handles information. In short: the app does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

1. What the app stores, and where

Your study progress (which kanji you have seen, your answers and review dates), your settings and your streak are saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We have no server and no account system, so we cannot see or access this information.

2. Purchases

Kanjilink Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment, receipts, taxes and refunds are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro is owned on your account; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show a kanji every few hours. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Set wallpaper: only if you turn on “Kanji on lock screen”, to place the current kanji on your lock screen.
  • Network access: used only by Google Play Billing for purchases.

4. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking.

5. Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Kanjilink → Storage → Clear data. This deletes all study progress from your phone. A Pro purchase stays linked to your Google account and can be restored with “Restore purchase”.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience study tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law and similar laws you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete; your data on your phone is under your control (section 5).

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

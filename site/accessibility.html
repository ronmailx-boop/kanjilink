Accessibility statement

Updated: 23 November 2026

We want Kanjilink and this website to be usable by everyone, and we aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA (Israeli Standard IS 5568).

What we have done

  • The app supports Android's font-size setting: text wraps instead of being cut, and buttons grow with the font.
  • Buttons have touch targets of at least 48 dp, and icon buttons have spoken labels for TalkBack.
  • Light and dark themes follow your phone, with colour contrast checked for readable text.
  • This site uses simple HTML with headings, real links and no content that depends on colour alone.

Known limits

Kanji are shown as large characters; the lock-screen wallpaper and widget glyph are images, so their readings and meanings are also shown as text next to them.

Contact

If something is hard to use, tell us: vplusstudio.app@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 14 days.
